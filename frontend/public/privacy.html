Privacy Policy

Bodha AI · Last updated 17 September 2026

Bodha AI is a personal project: an AI chat assistant with live web search. This page explains what information the app stores and how it is used.

Information we store

  • Account details: your username, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password (we never store the password itself).
  • Chats: the messages you send, the AI's replies, and chat titles, so you can come back to your conversations.
  • A login cookie: a secure, HTTP-only cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 24 hours. It is not used for tracking or advertising.

How your information is used

  • To create your account and verify your email address.
  • To generate replies to your messages and show your chat history.
  • To protect the service, for example by limiting repeated login attempts.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Services that process your information

  • Google Gemini and Mistral AI receive the messages in a chat in order to generate replies.
  • Tavily receives search queries when a question needs up-to-date information from the web.
  • Gmail (through the Gmail API) is used only to send the verification email to the address you sign up with. Bodha AI does not read, store, or access your Google account or mailbox.
  • MongoDB Atlas stores account and chat data, and Render hosts the application.

Your choices

  • You can rename or delete any chat from the app at any time.
  • To delete your account and all of its data, email the address below from the email you signed up with.
